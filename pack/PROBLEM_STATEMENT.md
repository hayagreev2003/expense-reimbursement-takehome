# Take-home: Travel Expense Reimbursement

**Deliverable: a working application, plus a short note. Timebox below.**

---

## The situation

Nortex Industries is a mid-size manufacturing company. When an employee travels for work, this is what actually happens.

Before the trip, they ask their manager for approval over email and request a travel advance. Finance releases the advance. Flights and hotels are booked through the travel desk, so confirmation mails land in the employee's inbox. On the ground they take cabs, eat meals, host the customer for dinner. Receipts arrive by email, some as text, some as photographs of paper bills.

When they get back, they open a blank Travel Expense Settlement Form and fill it in by hand from all of that. It takes 25–30 minutes and it is where most of the errors come from. Then the form goes up an approval chain, then to Finance, then the employee spends the next two weeks asking Finance where the money is.

We want to remove the 25 minutes, remove the errors, and remove the follow-ups.

## What we want you to build

A working application that a company like Nortex could actually put in front of its people.

We are not going to specify the screens, the stack, the database, or the architecture. Those are your decisions and we will judge them. Use whatever you are fastest in.

## What to send back

1. **A link to the running application**, or a repo with a one-command way to run it. If you deploy it somewhere, better.
2. **A short note (one page, no more)** covering: what you understood the problem to be, the assumptions you made, what you built and what you deliberately left out, and where it breaks.
3. **A 3–5 minute screen recording** walking through it end to end.

We have deliberately not specified everything. Do not wait for clarification. Make a call, and tell us in the note what you decided and why.

## Timebox

**48 hours from receipt.** We expect roughly 8 hours of actual work inside that. We would much rather see a smaller thing that works end to end and is honestly described, than a large thing that only runs on your laptop.

You are free to use AI coding tools. You will be asked to explain any line of what you submit.
